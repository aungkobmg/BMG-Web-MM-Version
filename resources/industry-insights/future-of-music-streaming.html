The Future of Music Streaming

From per-stream economics to superfan monetization: where music streaming is heading and what it means for independent artists in emerging markets.

Streaming is now the default

Streaming accounts for the majority of recorded-music revenue worldwide. The question is no longer whether to stream — it's how to make streaming work alongside new income layers.

Beyond the per-stream payout

Platforms are experimenting with fan-payout models, tipping, deluxe windows and direct-to-fan stores. Superfans, not raw play counts, increasingly drive artist income.

Emerging markets leapfrog

Myanmar and Southeast Asia skip legacy steps the way they skipped CDs — mobile-first listening, local-language playlists and short-form video discovery create outsized opportunities for local catalogs.

What artists should do now

Own your metadata, register everywhere, monetize user-generated content with Content ID, and treat every platform as one node in a diversified revenue graph.
