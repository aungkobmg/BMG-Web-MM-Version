How to Get Your Spotify Artist Profile Verified

A step-by-step guide to claiming your Spotify artist profile through a distributor like BMG and getting the blue verification badge.

What verification gets you

A verified profile gives you control of your artist page, access to Spotify for Artists analytics, playlist pitching and the ability to update your bio and imagery. Unverified profiles are managed by whoever's release metadata matched first — which may not be you.

Step 1: Release through a distributor

Spotify only allows artists to claim profiles via a distribution partner. When BMG delivers your first release, your artist page is created automatically and a claim link is added to your dashboard.

Step 2: Claim your profile

Follow the Spotify for Artists claim URL from your BMG dashboard. You'll be redirected to verify ownership. Approval usually takes a few minutes to 24 hours.

Step 3: Finish your profile

Upload your header image, bio, social links and pick your artist pick. A complete profile converts listeners into followers at a dramatically higher rate.
