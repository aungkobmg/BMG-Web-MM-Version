A Music Copyright Guide for Myanmar Artists

Musical composition vs sound recording rights, registration under Myanmar's Copyright Act, and how to collect royalties locally and abroad.

Two copyrights in every song

Every recording contains two separate rights: the musical composition (lyrics + melody, owned by songwriters/publishers) and the sound recording (the master, owned by the artist or label). Each earns different royalties from different uses.

Myanmar's Copyright Act

Myanmar's Copyright Act (2019) protects works automatically from creation — registration is not mandatory but depositing evidence of authorship helps enforce your rights. Protection generally lasts for the life of the author plus 50 years.

Collecting societies

Performance royalties (radio, TV, public playback) are collected by collecting societies. Because local infrastructure is still developing, international collection through publishing administration is essential for Myanmar artists.

Practical checklist

Split sheets per song, dated session files, ISRC codes per recording, ISWCs per composition, and registering works with BMG so nothing is missed when your music crosses borders.
